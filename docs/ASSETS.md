# Third-party assets and their licences

Every file in this repository that was not written for it is listed here with its source, its
licence and what that licence requires. **An asset that is not listed here must not be in the
repository.** The rules this list enforces come from `docs/ENGINE_DECISION.md` and
`docs/design/08_PRESENTATION.md` §9:

- Allowed to bundle: CC0, public-domain, CC BY (with attribution below), and the CMU Graphics Lab
  Motion Capture Database terms (use and redistribution permitted, not resale; acknowledgement given).
- **Not** allowed: Mixamo files (its licence forbids redistributing the files themselves), LAFAN1,
  Bandai Namco motion data, AMASS/SMPL/SMPL-X and derivatives (non-commercial), Ready Player Me
  avatars, and anything whose licence could not be verified at the time of download.
- No trademarks of real organisations. The broadcast *grammar* is modelled on televised MMA; no
  promotion's logos, names, fonts or graphics are reproduced.

Entry format (docs/design/08 §9.2): id, what it is, source URL, licence, attribution text if required,
files in this repo, and any modification made.

- **What**: the base body mesh (`base.obj`), body-shape morph targets (macro ethnicity × age,
  universal muscle × weight, height, proportions), the Mixamo-compatible 52-bone rig definition
  (`rig.mixamo.json`) and its skin weights (`weights.mixamo.json`).
- **Source**: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2 — `src/mpfb/data/`
- **Licence**: CC0 1.0 Universal (`LICENSE.ASSETS.md` in that repository). The MPFB2 *code* is GPL
  and is **not** used or shipped; only the data files are.
- **Attribution**: not required; given anyway: *Human base mesh, targets and rig from the MakeHuman
  project (MPFB2), CC0.*
- **Files**: `src/presentation/rig/rigData.ts` (generated by `scripts/assets/build-rig.mjs`: bone
  hierarchy and rest positions, converted to three.js coordinates). Body mesh assets are added by
  the character pipeline and listed below as they land.
- **Modifications**: coordinate conversion (Blender Z-up → three.js Y-up, facing +Z), bone roll
  dropped in favour of an identity-rest convention (see `src/presentation/rig/skeleton.ts`).

### body.mpfb2.runtime — the character module's body asset (derived from body.mpfb2.base)
- **What**: the runtime body the character module morphs and fits per fighter.
- **Source**: MPFB2 data as above, fetched from
  `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/master/src/mpfb/data/`:
  `3dobjs/base.obj`, `rigs/standard/rig.mixamo.json`, `rigs/standard/weights.mixamo.json`,
  380 targets under `targets/` (listed in `body.json` → `targets`; the selection is the `TARGETS`
  table in the script): `macrodetails/{african,asian,caucasian}-{male,female}-{young,old}`,
  `macrodetails/universal-*` (gender × age × muscle × weight),
  `macrodetails/height/*-averagemuscle-averageweight-{min,max}height`,
  `macrodetails/proportions/*-averagemuscle-averageweight-idealproportions`, regional head, face,
  neck, torso, stomach, arm and leg targets, and `expression/units/{african,asian,caucasian}/*`.
  Lookdev pass 2 added the eye (`eyes/{l,r}-eye-*`), nose, mouth, chin, cheek, forehead and brow
  targets the face presets use, and MPFB's 29 asymmetry pairs (`asym/asym-*-{l,r}`) for the
  seeded per-fighter asymmetry (`OPPOSITES` and the `asym` loop in the script).
- **Licence**: CC0 1.0 (as body.mpfb2.base). **Attribution**: *Human body derived from the MakeHuman
  project (MPFB2), CC0.*
- **Files** (generated by `scripts/assets/build-body.mjs`; deterministic, checksum in `body.json`):
  - `static/assets/body/body.json` (~100 KB) — header: counts, part ranges, bone order, joint
    references, target table, buffer table, SHA-256 of the binary.
  - `static/assets/body/body.bin` (5.96 MB) — A-pose positions, 4-bone skin weights (quantised to
    1/255, summing exactly to 255), body UVs, render→src map, LOD0/1/2 index buffers (LOD1/2 by
    `meshoptimizer` simplification of the welded mesh), helper-tights and upper-teeth triangles,
    and the 380 targets as int16 deltas (sparse or dense).
  - `static/assets/body/masks/mpfb_{lips,fingernails,toenails,aureolae,ears,eyelids,face,inside-mouth}.jpg`

    copied unmodified.
- **Modifications**: helper geometry (skirt, hair, genital, tongue, eyelash helpers, lower teeth)
  dropped; each `joint-*` cube and helper eye replaced by one virtual vertex at its mean (joint
  re-derivation is unchanged because a CUBE joint *is* that mean); quads split along the shorter
  diagonal; UV seams split; deltas quantised to int16 with a per-target scale; decimetres →
  metres. The procedural detail textures (pores, sweat beads, cloth, leather) are generated at
  runtime by `src/presentation/character/textures.ts` and are original.

## Motion capture

Real human motion, retargeted onto the canonical skeleton by `scripts/assets/build-mocap.mjs`
(`scripts/assets/mocap-lib.mjs`, `scripts/assets/retarget.mjs`) and read at runtime by
`src/presentation/assets/motionLibrary.ts`. Raw downloads live in the git-ignored `.cache/assets/`;
only the converted library is committed. Every clip in `static/assets/motion/manifest.json` names
its `asset` (an id below), `licence`, source `take` and frame range.

Common modifications (both sources): joint rotations retargeted from the source skeleton onto the
canonical 22-bone body (identity-rest convention; per-bone calibration from a neutral standing
pose, elbow/knee twist fitted to the measured hinge axes); root translation scaled by the leg-length
ratio; two-bone leg IK so the ankles follow the scaled source trajectory; resampled to 30 fps (CMU:
every 4th frame of 120 fps); each take rotated so its strike / travel points at +Z; loops cut at the
best-matching frame pair and their drift removed; quantised to int16; left/right-mirrored variants
generated at runtime for the stance that was not captured. No motion was keyframed or invented: every
frame is a transformed capture frame.
