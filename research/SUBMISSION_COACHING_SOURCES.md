## Rear naked choke
- Danaher: only "the width of your own wrist" needs to go under the chin; flattened-fist wedge enters behind
  the ear; second hand palm-down on the far shoulder; finish by rotating toward the choking side.
  https://bjjfanatics.com/blogs/news/the-perfect-rear-naked-choke-with-john-danaher
- Seatbelt power comes from elbow placement, not squeezing; diagonal control beats same-side; hand fighting
  low-energy and mechanical. https://bjjfanatics.com/blogs/news/basics-of-john-danaher-s-back-system
- Straight Jacket system = upper-body immobilisation that kills hand fighting. https://bjjgraph.org/Systems/Danaher-Straight-Jacket-System
- Figure-four vs palm-to-palm; chin tuck standard defence, countered by head pull-back or chin pressure;
  "always fight the choking arm"; ~8.9 s to LOC. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rear_naked_choke
- Short choke (Marcelo Garcia): gable grip, no figure-four; Digitsu logs 7 comp finishes (71% no-gi).
  https://digitsu.com/t/short-choke
- Escape: two-on-one on the choking arm from the moment the back is taken; tuck chin, pull arm down, turn to
  the side, peel wrist/elbow. https://evolve-mma.com/blog/how-to-escape-the-rear-naked-choke/
- Pulling hooks out frees mobility but exposes the neck. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back_mount
- FightScience (secondary, uncited stats): "critical 15 seconds" of grip stripping; five chin-tuck counters
  (forehead push, sawing forearm, nose-line, jaw compression, switch to armbar); MMA: gloves reduce
  defender grip fighting, GnP forces turtle/back exposure, sweat speeds sequences.
  https://fightscience.com/guides/rear-naked-choke-guide

## Guillotine variants and defences
- Marcelotine (high elbow, hand to opposite shoulder, elbows squeeze + hip thrust) vs arm-in are distinct.
  https://bjjfanatics.com/blogs/news/arm-in-guillotine-vs-high-elbow-guillotine-marcelotine
- Kesting guide: high-elbow, arm-in, five/ten-finger, front naked choke; guillotine as a control position
  first; entries from front headlock, sprawl, snapdown, half guard, mount. https://www.grapplearts.com/guillotine-choke/
- Strong side vs weak side: defender scrambles to the weak side (head one side, legs the other).
  https://www.grapplearts.com/strong-weak-side-guillotine-choke/
- Defence: far arm over the shoulder as high as possible, shoulder pressure into face, step over leg, rotate
  hips to the opposite side. https://evolve-mma.com/blog/bjj-101-the-guillotine-choke/
- MMA: posture up immediately, hand-fight, "drop and stack" to the hip then pass; Mir-Kongo UFC 107,
  Ortega-Swanson standing guillotine, Oliveira-Guida/Lee; std ~8.9 s, arm-in ~10.2 s.
  https://mmasucka.com/guides/guillotine-choke-the-complete-guide/
- Digitsu: 256 logged comp finishes, 74.6% no-gi. https://digitsu.com/t/guillotine-choke
- Von Flue: move to the weak side into side control, link hands trapping their arm, drive shoulder into the
  neck; Von Flue d. Karalexis UFN 3 (16 Jan 2006); OSP 4 wins. https://www.grapplearts.com/how-to-do-the-von-flue-choke/

## D'Arce / Anaconda / arm triangle
- Both trap head + one arm in RNC-style configuration, differ in arm placement; entries from front headlock,
  sprawl, turtle, half guard; finish by walking to the side or rolling.
  https://www.grapplearts.com/the-difference-between-the-brabo-choke-and-the-anaconda-choke-plus-tips-for-both/
- Brabo defence: stay low at the hip when grabbing the leg; if caught, go flat to your back before it locks;
  scramble to the correct side. https://www.grapplearts.com/brabo-choke-defense/
- Kata gatame from mount/side control (palm-to-palm); D'Arce perpendicular vs half-guard underhook;
  anaconda vs a shot, finished with gator roll; all three: arm on one carotid, opponent's shoulder on the
  other, bodyweight seals. https://evolve-mma.com/blog/3-arm-triangle-variations-you-need-in-your-bjj-arsenal/
- ~7.2 s to LOC. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arm_triangle_choke
- Arm-triangle escape: clasp palms, drive elbow into head, bridge toward the elbow, scoot back.
  https://www.bjjee.com/articles/this-simple-arm-triangle-escape-works-surprisingly-well/
- Chimaev D'Arce setups; Makhachev-Oliveira UFC 280 arm triangle; Japanese necktie chains with Peruvian.
  https://evolve-mma.com/blog/heres-how-to-do-the-japanese-necktie-in-bjj/

## Triangle
- Standard (90% of triangles), rear, side (yoko-sankaku), inverted, arm triangle.
  https://www.grapplearts.com/five-triangle-chokes-you-should-know/
- ~9.5 s; from guard, mount, side control, back; flying triangle; defence = head up, arm off the carotid,
  stand and walk to the opposite side; slams legal in MMA. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangle_choke
- Escapes: early - trapped-arm elbow bar across hip + posture; late - post under armpit, lock hands on hip,
  stand and step over; posture up (hands on belly, look at ceiling); arm under + jump to side control.
  https://evolve-mma.com/blog/5-must-know-triangle-escapes-for-your-bjj-game/
- Defences: posture, stack, step-over, "Rampage slam"; chains: wristlock, armbar, kimura, omoplata; Paul
  Craig most UFC triangle finishes. https://fightscience.com/guides/triangle-choke-setup-system
- Digitsu: 664 logged comp finishes, 68.5% gi. https://digitsu.com/t/triangle-choke
- Every triangle setup is an omoplata setup. https://www.grapplearts.com/every-triangle-choke-setup-is-also-an-omoplata-setup/
- Slams: Rampage-Arona Pride 2004 powerbomb KO; Hughes-Newton UFC 34.
- **CORRECTION**: hitchhiker is an armbar escape, not a triangle escape. No verified flying-triangle MMA
  finish found in text.

## Armbar
- Attacks: mount (trap arm, rotate, step over head), guard cutting armbar, KOB "shotgun", back control (when
  they grab the RNC arm), from kimura trap. https://evolve-mma.com/blog/the-top-5-ways-to-attack-with-the-armbar-from-different-positions/
- Finishing vs resisting opponent: defenders lock hands (palm-to-palm, chain, RNC grip); ~20 grip breaks;
  alternatives side triangle, biceps slicer. https://www.grapplearts.com/how-to-finish-the-armbar-against-a-resisting-opponent/
- Hitchhiker escape: thumb toward their legs, elbow bent, roll past their hips; fails once the arm is
  straight or if they switch to triangle. https://evolve-mma.com/blog/what-is-the-hitchhiker-escape-in-bjj/
- Slam counters: AUFC 40 armbar ended by slams; Senegalese wrestler powerbombs kimura.
- **CORRECTION**: no text source for Hendricks slamming out of an armbar; Rousey's page lists no slam escapes
  (Zingano 14 s, Gomes 25 s, Tweet 49 s).
- Flying armbars: Rumina Sato-Charles Taylor (Shooto, 6 s), DJ-Borg UFC 216 (suplex-to-armbar),
  Waterson-Califano (15 s), Namajunas-Catron (12 s). **CORRECTION**: Edwards/Rashad not examples.

## Kimura
- Kimura as a position; back take from half guard; north-south kimura; "two grips = 90% of finishes".
  https://www.grapplearts.com/mastering-the-kimura/ https://www.grapplearts.com/the-kimura-as-a-position/
- Mir-Nogueira UFC 140 breakdown. https://www.grapplearts.com/frank-mirs-kimura-on-antonio-rodrigo-nogueira-at-ufc-140/
- Five counters: spin to back vs closed-guard kimura; butterfly lift vs half-guard top kimura; T-kimura
  reversal; staple bottom leg and scoot; spin armbar/back take vs bottom side-control kimura.
  https://www.grapplearts.com/5-kimura-counters/
- Defences: elbow separation, straighten arm, roll; kimura as a sweep engine; Kimura Trap System (Avellan) -
  grip is the constant through transitions; strong counter to the single leg.
  https://fightscience.com/guides/kimura-technique-guide https://www.bjjee.com/articles/kimura-trap-most-powerful-counter-to-the-single-leg-takedown/

## Americana
- Figure-four, arm at 90 deg, pin elbow and wrist, lift elbow / drive wrist; side control best, also mount; if
  arm straightens switch to straight armlock. https://evolve-mma.com/blog/bjj-101-the-americana/ (no MMA
  finish-rate source found).

## Omoplata
- Entries, fixes; best defence = low-angle cartwheel landing in side control; stopping the forward-roll
  escape. https://www.grapplearts.com/the-best-omoplata-defense/
- Rare in MMA: tricky setup, slips when sweaty. https://www.bjjee.com/articles/these-are-the-rarest-and-the-most-difficult-submissions-in-mma/

## Heel hooks
- Danaher system: inside position, single/double/cross ashi garami, inside/outside heel hook, breaking
  mechanics. https://bjjfanatics.com/products/leglocks-enter-the-system-by-john-danaher
- Giles: bend in the knee essential (straight leg pulls out); hip pressure on the knee + upper-body control
  of the foot; 50/50 capture: gable grip on heel, toes pinched, forearm blade as lever.
  https://bjjfanatics.com/blogs/news/keep-the-knee-line-and-dominate-the-legs-with-lachlan-giles
- Craig Jones: butterfly entry, chin tucked vs crossface, legs open for mobility, secure total control
  before finishing. https://bjjfanatics.com/blogs/news/learn-how-to-heel-hook-like-craig-jones
- Inside/inverted most powerful; positions 50/50, inside/outside sankaku, ashi garami; defences: hide the
  heel, clear the foot; rolling away tightens it. https://evolve-mma.com/blog/the-advanced-and-complete-guide-to-the-bjj-heel-hook/
- Escape guide: connect feet before they grab the heel; clear the knee line ASAP; turn sole toward the
  opponent, point toes; then control arms, sprawl. https://evolve-mma.com/blog/the-bjj-leg-lock-escape-guide/
- Five positions: standard ashi, outside ashi, 50/50 (mutual access), reap, 411/saddle (best control).
  https://www.grapplearts.com/how-to-get-started-with-leglocks/
- MMA debate: Sonnen "don't work" vs Danaher "if utilised well at the appropriate time"; strangles end fights
  unconditionally, a tough opponent can fight through a torn knee; Valter Walker four straight.
- Ryan Hall heel hook of Penn UFC 232 (2018). **CORRECTION**: Maynard (2016) and Elkins (2019) were not heel
  hook finishes.

## Kneebar / ankle lock / toe hold
- Kneebar: knee against chest, extend hips, control the heel; common fault too far away.
  https://fightscience.com/guides/how-to-do-a-kneebar-in-bjj
- Straight ankle lock = foot in armpit, forearm on Achilles, hip drive; toe hold = figure-four near toes.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leglock
- Straight ankle lock one of the rarest MMA finishes (well-defended, low injury threat).

## Rare / named submissions (verified MMA instances)
- North-south choke: gable grip, sprawl hips, drop shoulder; ~9.4 s; Monson 17 MMA wins with it; Merab
  vs O'Malley UFC 316. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North%E2%80%93south_choke
- Ezekiel: Oleinik vs Pesta UFC FN 103 (Jan 2017) from bottom of mount, first in UFC; vs Albini UFC 224.
- Peruvian necktie: Dollaway vs Taylor (2008, first) and vs Doerksen UFC 119. **CORRECTION**: not Ferguson/Garbrandt.
- Bulldog: **CORRECTION**: Askren DID beat Lawler by bulldog choke UFC 235 (R1 3:20, disputed stoppage);
  Medeiros reverse bulldog UFC 177.
- Twister: Jung vs Garcia UFN 24 (2011, R2 4:59); Mitchell vs Sayles (2019); Blackshear (2023/24).
  Neck cranks banned IBJJF/amateur MMA, legal pro MMA.
- Gogoplata: Diaz-Gomi Pride 33 (NC); Aoki-Hansen Pride Shockwave 2006 R1 2:24; mounted gogoplata vs Nagata.
- Calf slicer: **CORRECTION**: Oliveira vs Wisely, UFC on Fox (Jan 2012) R1 1:43, first in UFC (not Elkins).
- Crucifix: chokes and neck cranks from perpendicular back control of both arms.

## Why elite fighters rarely tap / MMA-specific factors
- Gloves hinder defender's grip fighting; GnP forces turtle/back exposure; sweat/cage speed sequences.
- No-gi chokes (guillotine/RNC/anaconda/arm-triangle/D'Arce) favoured: no cloth grips, survive strikes.
- "67% of UFC submission wins are chokes" vs ADCC 39% leg locks; sport BJJ overtrains joint locks and
  guard pulling. https://www.bjjee.com/videos/reasons-for-sport-jiu-jitsus-recent-failure-in-mma/
- Bravo: guard game weak in MMA. Adesanya: guillotine's cost = ending up underneath (positional risk drives
  early release). Sweat + defence knowledge kill omoplata/ankle-lock finishes.
