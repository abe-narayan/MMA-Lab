## 3. Heel hook / leg lock trend
- UFC heel hooks all-time: 24 [E, FightAlpha Jun 2026]; kneebar 20; toe hold 1; calf slicer 3;
  Suloev stretch 3 → leg-lock family ≈ 51/1,695 ≈ 3.0% [Est]; Grappler HQ 3.3% [S].
- Verified heel hooks by year (partial): 2018:1 (Ryan Hall d. Penn), 2020:1 (Hooper), 2024:1
  (Walker d. Tafa), 2025:3 (Walker ×3). Palhares ×4 (2010–2013). Walker record 4 consecutive.
- ADCC 2024: 125 matches, 53 subs (42%); heel hooks 4/53 (7.5%); lower-body subs 22% vs upper 78%;
  chokes 65%, arm attacks 20%. Leg locks 28–30% of ADCC finishes 2016–2022, 22% in 2024.
  https://www.bjjheroes.com/editorial/adcc-2024-after-math-data-compilation-and-analysis
